A vision beyond ordinary

“Trinetra” is Sanskrit for the three-eyed one — a vision that sees beyond the obvious.

And that’s exactly how we think.

Business problems rarely come with a flashing warning sign. Sometimes, the real opportunity is hiding in the data, the numbers or the details everyone else overlooks.

At Trinetra Consultancy, we bring together AI-powered insights and MBA-trained thinking to help businesses see the bigger picture — and act on it.

With expertise across Finance, Marketing and Operations, we turn complex business challenges into clearer decisions, smarter strategies and practical solutions.

Because better decisions shouldn’t come with a traditional consulting price tag.

Our Mission

Making business intelligence accessible, not exclusive.

To make high-quality business intelligence and actionable insights accessible to growing businesses in India and around the world — so they can make smarter moves, compete confidently and grow beyond the obvious.
